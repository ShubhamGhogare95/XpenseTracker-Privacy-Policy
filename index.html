Privacy Policy

App: Expense Tracker

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Expense Tracker is an offline expense-tracking application. Your expense records are intended to remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to export them.

1. About Expense Tracker

Expense Tracker helps you record and analyze personal expenses. The app provides expense entry, a ledger, calendar views, spending analysis, category breakdowns, spending trends, and CSV export.

2. Information We Collect

Expense information that you enter into the app, such as date, category, and amount, is stored locally on your device for the purpose of providing the app's expense-tracking functionality.

The app does not intentionally collect or transmit your expense records to a developer-operated server.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information entered into Expense Tracker is used locally to display expenses, calculate spending summaries, organize transactions, and provide weekly, monthly, and yearly spending analytics.

4. Data Sharing

Expense Tracker does not sell or share your expense records with third parties through a developer-operated service.

When you use CSV export, the app creates an export file on your device. Any subsequent sharing or transmission of that file is initiated and controlled by you through your device or another application.

5. Advertising and Analytics

XpenseTracker does not contain advertisements and does not intentionally include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs for tracking your activity.

6. Internet and Network Use

XpenseTracker is designed for offline expense tracking. The app does not require an online account or a developer-operated server to record and analyze expenses.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored expense information remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove the stored data using your device's controls.

CSV files that you export are separate files managed by you on your device. You are responsible for deleting exported files when you no longer need them.

8. Data Security

Expense Tracker does not transmit your expense records to a developer-operated server. Because data is stored locally, you should use the security features provided by your device, such as a screen lock, to help protect access to the device and its data.

9. Children's Privacy

Expense Tracker is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Financial Services Disclaimer

Expense Tracker is an expense-recording and spending-analysis tool. It does not provide banking, payment, lending, investment, insurance, cryptocurrency, or other financial services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
