Privacy Policy

App: Expense Tracker

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Expense Tracker provides offline expense tracking and optional cloud synchronization. When you use account login and cloud backup features, certain account and expense information is processed and stored using Firebase services. The app also displays advertisements through third-party advertising services.

1. About Expense Tracker

Expense Tracker is a personal expense management application developed by Shubham Ghogare. It helps users record daily expenses, manage budgets, organize transactions, and analyze their spending habits.

The application may provide the following features:

  • Daily expense recording and transaction management.
  • Expense categories and optional transaction details.
  • Monthly budget management.
  • Spending analysis, charts, and reports.
  • Google Sign-In and Email/Password authentication.
  • Cloud synchronization and backup using Firebase.
  • Offline expense tracking.
  • CSV and other supported data export features.
  • Advertisements provided by third-party advertising services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Expense Tracker may collect, process, or store the following categories of information.

2.1 Account Information

When you register or sign in using Firebase Authentication, we may process:

  • Email address associated with your account.
  • Firebase user ID (UID).
  • Name and profile picture, if made available through Google Sign-In and accessed by the app.
  • Authentication and account-related information.

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate your identity. We do not collect your Google password.

2.2 Expense and Financial Information

When you use expense tracking and cloud synchronization features, the following information may be stored:

  • Expense amounts and transaction dates.
  • Expense categories.
  • Optional transaction descriptions and notes.
  • Budget information and spending limits.
  • Currency preferences.
  • Other expense-related information entered by you.

This information is used to provide expense management, synchronization, reporting, and analytical features.

2.3 Advertising and Device Information

The application displays advertisements using third-party advertising services. Depending on the advertising provider, SDK configuration, and your device privacy settings, these services may process information such as:

  • Advertising ID or other advertising identifiers.
  • IP address.
  • Device type, operating system, and device information.
  • App interaction and advertisement interaction information.
  • Advertising performance and diagnostic information.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address, where applicable.

Advertising providers may use this information to deliver, measure, and improve advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information processed by the application may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Save and synchronize expense records across supported devices.
  • Display transaction history and financial summaries.
  • Generate budget reports, charts, and spending analysis.
  • Maintain application functionality and security.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Respond to support requests and account deletion requests.

4. Firebase Services and Cloud Storage

Expense Tracker uses Google Firebase services for authentication and cloud synchronization.

Depending on the features enabled, these services include:

  • Firebase Authentication: Used for Google Sign-In and Email/Password authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore: Used to store and synchronize expense records and associated account information.

Cloud records are associated with the authenticated user's account. We use Firebase security rules and appropriate access controls to restrict access to user-specific information.

When you use guest or offline mode, information may remain stored locally on your device until you use a cloud synchronization feature or otherwise transfer or export that information.

Firebase services are provided by Google. Their processing of information is also subject to Google's applicable privacy terms.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Advertisements and Third-Party Advertising Services

Expense Tracker displays advertisements using third-party advertising services.

If Google AdMob is used in the application, Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, device information, IP addresses, and advertisement interaction information to provide and measure advertisements.

Depending on applicable laws, user consent, and advertising settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

We do not directly receive your advertising account credentials. Advertising data processing is subject to the applicable advertising provider's privacy policies and settings.

Google advertising information: Google Ads Privacy Information

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information or expense records. However, certain information may be processed by third-party service providers when required to operate application features.

These services may include:

  • Google Firebase Authentication for account authentication.
  • Google Cloud Firestore for cloud data storage and synchronization.
  • Google Sign-In for authentication.
  • Third-party advertising providers for displaying and measuring advertisements.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and the services they provide.

We do not authorize advertising providers to access the contents of your expense records merely for displaying advertisements.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Expense information may be stored locally on your device and, when cloud synchronization is enabled, on Firebase Cloud Firestore.

Account information may be retained while your account remains active and as necessary to provide application services.

When an account deletion request is successfully processed, associated account information and cloud expense records will be deleted, subject to any applicable legal or security retention requirements.

Any data that must be retained for a legitimate legal or security purpose will be retained only for the applicable retention period.

Locally stored information and exported CSV or backup files may remain on your device even after cloud account deletion. Users should separately remove these local files when necessary.

8. Data Security

We use appropriate security measures to protect information processed by the application.

These measures may include:

  • Firebase authentication for account access.
  • Firebase security rules to restrict access to user-specific data.
  • Encrypted network connections for data transmitted through Firebase services.
  • Appropriate access controls for cloud-stored information.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request permanent deletion of their Expense Tracker account and associated cloud data.

To request account deletion:

  1. Send an email to shubhamghogare.2011@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Expense Tracker - Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your registered account.
  4. We may request additional information to verify account ownership.

After successful verification, the account and associated cloud-stored information, including expense records and account profile information, will be deleted in accordance with our deletion procedures.

Requests will be processed within 30 days after successful verification, unless additional time is required by applicable law or for legitimate security reasons. If additional time is required, the user will be informed.

Information stored locally on your device and exported backup files may need to be deleted separately.

Account deletion is permanent and deleted cloud information may not be recoverable.
